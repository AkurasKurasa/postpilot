The crew

One orchestrator, three subagents, one rulebook, one human.

Each agent is a role, a skill file and a prompt. The orchestrator decides who works next. Governance is plain code so a model can't argue its way past it.

The gate · approval.html

Nothing is posted until the owner says so.

Every draft arrives as PENDING. Flagged posts sit at the top. Edits are re-checked against the hard rules as you type, and approving a post that still breaks a rule takes a second, logged click.

Output · final.html

The approved content calendar.

The server returns only approved posts from GET /api/final. Metrics come straight from the audit log, which is your evidence for the NIST MEASURE function.

Why a humanWhere: after the Reviewer and before the final output, for every post. Why: posts speak publicly for the business. A wrong price, a false claim or an offensive caption costs money and reputation, and the AI can't know the owner's real context: stock levels, local events, customer sensitivities. The owner stays accountable.
